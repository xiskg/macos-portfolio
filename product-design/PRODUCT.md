# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user's request to build freely: buildless HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with local asset preparation. A portfolio with video, readable imagery and browser-local interactions does not need an application server or accounts.

## Users

International recruiters and product/design hiring managers evaluating João de Almeida for remote mid-level Product Design and UI/UX roles. Evaluation must work on desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

Present one substantial FastGovBid project with persuasive, legible proof of João's interface and interaction design. Make the case, resume and contact immediately accessible.

## Positioning

The work combines a background in brand/visual design with responsibility for complex B2B product flows. The portfolio follows a bid across search, evaluation, pursuit tracking and authoring, then examines three decisions backed by artifacts.

## Operating Context

Recruiters need an overview; design leads need decisions, concessions, responsibility and implementation context. The user likes the approved English copy and connected 60-second film, wants examples/screenshots, and delegates the visual decisions to the assistant.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Home, detailed case, expandable interface images, on-demand video, an isolated interactive search-rule study, design acceptance notes, anchor navigation, resume and contact. No backend or authentication required in the portfolio itself. On October 25, the owner explicitly authorized public access to the portfolio and resume. This repository integrates that portfolio at dealmeida.me/product-design alongside the macOS experience.

## Brand Commitments

João de Almeida. English copy approved in the parent project. FastGovBid designs remain recognizable and are shown as their own product; the portfolio may establish a separate presentation system. No invented metrics, interviews, testimonials or shipped capabilities.

## Evidence on Hand

Approved manuscripts in `content/`; native interface screenshots, sanitized demonstration film and resume in `assets/`. The design and image/font manifests preserve provenance. The private research and original motion source remain in the separate authoring workspace. Source data and design proposals remain distinguishable from implementation.

## Product Principles

- Let the designed interfaces lead the reading.
- Explain concrete choices next to the evidence.
- Keep navigation, contact and resume direct.
- Preserve readable detail on phones and for people who do not play the video.
- Credit individual design responsibility and engineering collaboration accurately.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus, descriptive image alternatives, expandable screenshots, contrast, reduced motion and full mobile content.
