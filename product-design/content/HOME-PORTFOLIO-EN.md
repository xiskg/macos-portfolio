## How I work

I pay attention to how people keep their place: filters that carry across search views, drafts that show what remains, and shared files with clear access. I bring that same care to typography, layout and components.

Figma has been my daily design tool for five years. For FastGovBid, the interface work shown here comes from Paper, with Tailwind exports, ClickUp discussions and GitHub collaboration supporting engineering handoff.
